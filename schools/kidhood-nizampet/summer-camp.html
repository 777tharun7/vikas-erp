A Summer Full of Fun, Friends & Discovery

Give your child an unforgettable vacation packed with colorful giant bubbles, science discovery, splash play, and creative arts in Nizampet.

🎪 Non-Stop Vacation Magic

What Your Child Will Experience at Camp

Summer shouldn't mean spending endless hours glued to mobile screens and television. Kidhood’s annual Summer Camp transforms the holidays into an exhilarating playground of experiential learning, laughter, and lifelong friendships.

From giant outdoor rainbow bubbles and splash pool days to hands-on clay pottery and child-friendly science experiments, every day is designed to foster curiosity, athletic coordination, and team camaraderie.

🫧

Outdoor Games

Water splash, lawn obstacle courses & giant bubbles.

🔬

Junior Science

Volcano foam, seed germination & shadow puppets.

🎨 4 Thrilling Exploration Zones

Designed for Toddlers, Preschoolers & Early Primary

Divided into age-appropriate tracks (2–4 yrs, 5–7 yrs, and 8–10 yrs) so every child has an engaging time.

🎨

Pottery & Canvas Art

Terracotta clay modeling, tie-dye shirts, finger canvas painting, and paper mache creations that kids take home proudly.

🤖

Robotics & STEM Blocks

Lego engineering, circuit puzzles, magnetic tiles, and simple motorized robots sparking young problem-solvers.

💦

Splash Pool Fridays

Supervised shallow water fun with floating rubber ducks, mini water slides, and chilled fresh fruit ice-pops.

🎭

Music & Drama Choir

Costume roleplay, musical percussion circles, storytelling theatrics, and public speaking rehearsals.
