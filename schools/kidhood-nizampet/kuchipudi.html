Let Your Child Discover the Joy of Classical Dance

Kuchipudi is one of India's most expressive classical dance forms originating right here in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana. At Kidhood Nizampet, we introduce children as young as 4 years old to this rich heritage in an encouraging, fun-filled setting.

Under the guidance of patient, accredited dance gurus, children master rhythmic footwork (Adavus), delicate hand gestures (Hastas / Mudras), and facial expressions (Bhavas).

🎵 Structured Curriculum

What Children Learn in Our Kuchipudi Batches

A step-by-step classical curriculum structured specifically for young developing bodies and minds.

👐

Asamyukta & Samyukta Hastas

Learning single-hand and combined hand gestures depicting peacocks, flowers, nature, and storytelling characters.

🦶

Basic Adavus (Steps)

Rhythmic stamping patterns synchronized with traditional Sanskrit and Telugu beats (Tha-Dhi-Thom-Nam).

🎭

Abhinaya & Facial Expressions

Expressing joy, wonder, and devotion through graceful eye, eyebrow, and smile coordination.

🎶

Carnatic Music Basics

Training musical ear, rhythm sensibility, and singing along with classical dance slokas.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Kuchipudi Dance FAQ

What is the minimum age to enroll in Kuchipudi?
▼
Children can join starting from 4 years old. The curriculum for ages 4 to 6 is designed with gentle stretches, playful hand gestures, and rhythm games.
Are performance costumes provided for stage shows?
▼
Yes! During our annual festivals and cultural showcases, the school coordinates traditional classical dance costumes, jewelry, and stage makeup.
Can daycare children attend Kuchipudi classes?
▼
Absolutely. Many parents who enroll their child in our extended daycare take advantage of the evening Kuchipudi classes without needing an extra commute.
