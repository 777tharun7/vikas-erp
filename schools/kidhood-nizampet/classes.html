1.5 – 3 Yrs

Toddler Program

The first stretch away from home is a big one. Our toddler room is built for gentle sensory exploration, warm separation routines, and small-group interaction.

  • ✓ Focus: Sensory & Emotional Development
  • ✓ Learning: Play-Based Activities & Story Circles
  • ✓ Schedule: 9:30 AM – 12:30 PM (Mon–Fri)
3 – 4 Yrs

Nursery Program

Children begin building early language and number sense through stories and guided play, at a pace that respects how young children absorb new concepts.

  • ✓ Focus: Phonics, Spatial Recognition & Colors
  • ✓ Learning: Puppet Stories & Tactile Math Blocks
  • ✓ Schedule: 9:00 AM – 1:00 PM (Mon–Fri)
4 – 5 Yrs

Lower Kindergarten (LKG)

LKG focuses on reading readiness and early problem-solving skills, layered into enjoyable group activities rather than isolated rote worksheets.

  • ✓ Focus: Sight Words, Sentence Building & Addition
  • ✓ Learning: Interactive Logic Trays & Science Tables
  • ✓ Schedule: 9:00 AM – 1:30 PM (Mon–Fri)
5 – 6 Yrs

Upper Kindergarten (UKG)

Our UKG program prepares children for the shift into primary school — stronger literacy, basic numeracy, and the confidence to work independently.

  • ✓ Focus: Primary School Readiness & Writing Fluency
  • ✓ Learning: Show & Tell, Reasoning & Teamwork
  • ✓ Schedule: 9:00 AM – 2:00 PM (Mon–Fri)
