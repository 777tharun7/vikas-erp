❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Answers for Thoughtful Parents

Everything you need to know about enrollment, security, timings and curriculum.

What programs are offered at Kidhood Nizampet?
▼
Kidhood is a preschool and daycare in Nizampet offering Toddler, Nursery, LKG and UKG programs for children aged 1.5 to 6 years, along with daycare and Kuchipudi dance classes.
What is the minimum age for admission?
▼
Children can join from 1.5 years old in our Toddler program, through to 6 years in Upper Kindergarten.
Does Kidhood provide daycare for working parents?
▼
Yes. Kidhood Nizampet runs a supervised daycare alongside its preschool programs for parents who need extended-hours care, from 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM on weekdays.
What extracurricular activities are available?
▼
Kuchipudi dance and singing classes, along with Kung Fu martial arts and holiday summer camps.
What is Kidhood's approach to early learning?
▼
Kidhood uses a play-based learning approach, introducing literacy, numeracy and social skills through stories, hands-on activities and guided play rather than rote memorization.
